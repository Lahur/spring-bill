vault deployed to namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} as "{{ .Values.service.name }}".

Other apps in this namespace can reach it at:

  VAULT_ADDR=http://{{ .Values.service.name }}:8200
  VAULT_TOKEN={{ .Values.token }}

To seed secrets for an app (kv-v2 is enabled at "secret/"):

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec -it deploy/{{ .Values.service.name }} -- sh
  export VAULT_ADDR=http://127.0.0.1:8200
  export VAULT_TOKEN={{ .Values.token }}

  vault kv put secret/<spring.application.name> \
    some.key="value"
